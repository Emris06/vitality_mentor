# 03 — Solutions

> Source section: 4 of the original AI-Mentor-HUB project overview. Each subsection maps back to a numbered problem in [01-overview-and-problem.md](01-overview-and-problem.md).

---

## Problem 1 → Bank Operations Simulator

A full dummy-data simulator mirroring real bank interfaces (Colvir/YABS):

- 100% artificially generated training data
- Scenarios: Open Account, Deposit, Transfer, KYC
- Zero connection to real client data or live systems
- Eliminates all privacy and security risks while enabling real-skill training

## Problem 2 → HR Dashboard + Mentor Matching

- Real-time view of employee availability and workload
- Algorithm matches free employees to incoming newcomers as mentors
- Daily progress and module completion tracking
- Automatic result export to iSpring LMS

## Problem 3 → AI Knowledge Base + Q&A Assistant

- 24/7 availability, no waiting for a human
- Supports **Uzbek and Russian** languages
- Powered by **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** on internal bank documents
- Answers questions about regulations, dress code, procedures, SOPs
- Response time: ≤ 2 seconds

## Problem 4 → Guided Training Modules + Step-by-Step AI Support

- Structured learning path: Accounts → Deposits → KYC → AML
- AI explains what to do and why at every step
- Exercises, quizzes, and real-scenario simulations
- Immediate feedback and scoring

## Problem 5 → Gamification + Automated Skill Tracking

- Progress bars, points, and badges for completed modules
- Client communication simulator with virtual clients
- Visual skill gap map per employee
- Onboarding deadline counter
