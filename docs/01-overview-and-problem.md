# 01 — Executive Summary & Problem

> Source sections: 1, 2 of the original AI-Mentor-HUB project overview.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

AI-Mentor HUB is a unified onboarding, knowledge base, and skills analysis platform for banks. It combines:

- AI Knowledge Base (Track 1)
- Safe Bank-Operations Simulator with dummy data
- HR Dashboard + Mentor Matching
- Gamified Learning + Skill Tracking (Track 3)
- iSpring LMS Integration

All three tracks are served by one integrated platform, reducing HR workload, accelerating onboarding, and eliminating security risks.

---

## 2. The Problem

### One Clear Problem Statement

> Newcomers cannot safely learn real banking operations, and HR cannot manage onboarding effectively because there is no secure, structured training platform with AI guidance, mentor matching, and simulation of bank systems.

### Problem Breakdown

| #   | Problem                                                    | Why It Matters                                                                       |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Newcomers cannot access real banking systems (Colvir/YABS) | Privacy risks, data leaks, legal violations (Uzbekistan law / GDPR), potential fraud |
| 2   | HR cannot manage onboarding efficiently                    | No centralized system, manual tracking, no mentor visibility                         |
| 3   | Employees waste time answering the same basic questions    | Reduces productivity, creates inconsistent training quality                          |
| 4   | Newcomers don't understand complex bank procedures         | One mistake can cause financial or legal consequences                                |
| 5   | Onboarding is expensive and slow                           | HR and mentors cannot scale; delays hiring results                                   |
